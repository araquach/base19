Summertime is brilliant for us. For our hair? Not always quite so much.

Sunshine, plus chlorine, then sea water and more time outdoors can all leave their mark and by September, colour can feel a little faded and ends can start to feel drier than they did at the beginning of summer.

That is why Autumn is one of our favourite times for a hair reset.

It does not have to mean a dramatic transformation or automatically swapping blonde for brunette as soon as September arrives. Sometimes a fresh cut, a change in tone or simply putting some condition back into the hair is enough to make everything feel good again.

**Start With the Condition**

Before thinking about a new autumn colour, start with how your hair actually feels.

Sun exposure, chlorine, sea water, heat and styling can all take their toll over summer, leaving hair feeling drier, duller or more fragile by September. So before we start talking about richer brunettes and autumn blondes, it might be time to give your hair some attention first.

This is where a K18 treatment can come in.

K18 uses patented peptide technology designed to help reconnect polypeptide chains within the hair that can become damaged through chemical services, heat and other stresses. Rather than simply making the outside of the hair feel conditioned, it works within the hair structure to help repair damage.

Paired with a fresh cut to remove tired ends, a K18 treatment can be a great way to start your autumn hair reset and get your hair feeling stronger and healthier before heading into the new season.

And if you are planning a bigger colour change, speak to your stylist about the condition of your hair first. Healthy hair should always be the foundation of a great colour.

**Does Autumn Hair Have to Mean Going Darker?**

Definitely not.

There is something about September that makes us want a change, but an autumn hair colour does not have to mean abandoning your blonde or making a huge transformation. Sometimes it is simply about making your existing colour feel a little richer and more dimensional.

Blondes can move towards warmer, softer or more lived in tones. Brunettes can introduce depth, shine and subtle ribbons of colour. Reds and coppers can become richer and more vibrant, while a gloss or toner can completely refresh hair without a major colour change.

The aim is not to follow a seasonal rule. It is to find a colour that works with your hair, your skin tone and the way you actually want to wear it.

**Bring Back the Gloss**

If you still love your colour but it has lost some of its shine over summer, you might not need to start again.

![Base Colour Image](https://35fba853288929fc2c78-69cb25cc8c90ed23a3699fb2b2ac841c.ssl.cf5.rackcdn.com/The%20Autumn%20Hair%20Reset/base%20colour.jpg) 

A gloss or toner can be a brilliant way to refresh your existing hair colour, adjust the tone and bring back that freshly coloured finish. It is also ideal if you fancy a subtle seasonal shift without committing to a completely different look.

Pair that with a healthy trim and some attention to the condition and suddenly hair that felt a little tired can feel completely refreshed.

**Rethink Your Haircare for Autumn**

A seasonal reset is also a good opportunity to look at what you are doing at home.

As the weather changes and we move towards colder months and central heating, your hair may need something different from the products you were using throughout summer.

Your stylist can recommend products based on your individual hair rather than whatever happens to be trending online. That might mean introducing more moisture, protecting your colour, adding heat protection or using an at home treatment such as K18 to support the work you are having done in the salon.

Good hair does not only happen in the salon. What you do between appointments makes a difference too.
